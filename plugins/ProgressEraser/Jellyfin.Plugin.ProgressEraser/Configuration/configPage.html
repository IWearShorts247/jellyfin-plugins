Progress Eraser

Adds an eraser button next to Favorite and Mark Played on media cards, and to the item page. Clicking it clears your playback position for that item, removing it from Continue Watching. Played state and play count are not changed. On a series, season or collection it resets every in-progress episode inside.

Changes to the first two options take effect after a hard refresh of the web client. The injection option takes effect after a server restart.
